Questions

Good to know.

Which flooring option is best for me?

We'll go over that during your in-person consultation. Every home and style is different, and we'll help you find the right fit for how you live and what you want to spend.

Do you warranty your work?

Yes. Every job comes with a workmanship warranty.

Is sanding hardwood floors a DIY project?

Sanding hardwood takes professional skill and equipment to get it right. We recommend leaving it to a pro so the floor isn't damaged.

Can you help with design and color choices?

Absolutely. We keep up with current flooring trends and can help you pick designs and colors that suit your space.

How do I schedule an appointment?

Call us at 406-412-4592 or fill out the form below, and we'll get you on the schedule.

How do I care for my new floors?

We'll give you care instructions for your specific floor. In general, sweep or vacuum regularly, mop occasionally, and avoid too much moisture or harsh chemicals.
